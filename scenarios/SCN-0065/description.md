# Illuminator community energy balance with battery and hydrogen storage

Demonstrates the Illuminator mosaik-based kit by simulating whether a community can balance its electricity demand with local renewables and short- and long-term storage.

The Raspberry Pi deployment assigns the energy management controller to a leader and the component models to followers. The controller uses the battery first and activates electrolysis or the fuel cell when needed. The paper reports outcomes over a month using June profiles.
