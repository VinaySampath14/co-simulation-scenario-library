# Blockchain-based local electricity market on a distribution feeder

Evaluates a local electricity market that rewards local generation and coordinates transactions on a simulated distribution feeder.

Mosaik couples a household demand simulator, PV profiles, a distribution power-flow simulator and an Ethereum market adapter. The paper reports feeder demand, PV generation and market prices for the demonstration. Section III-C reports a public demo, but the historical repository could not be verified. [Author manuscript, Sections II–III](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9hf8d9hn/qt9hf8d9hn.pdf)
