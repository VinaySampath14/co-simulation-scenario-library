# GridSurfer EV charging in rural and urban distribution grids

Evaluates electric vehicle charging strategies using a prototype of mosaik in rural and urban distribution-grid configurations.

The GridSurfer experiment couples Python/SimPy EVs, MATLAB/Simulink photovoltaics, residential demand from CSV time series, and single-phase grid power flow using Python/Pylon. The authors report a prototype with fixed execution order and data mappings. [PDF pp. 9–10, Section 5 and Figure 7](https://uol.de/f/2/dept/informatik/ag/ui/publications/Schuette_Scherfke_Sonnenschein_2012_mosaik_-_Smart_Grid_Simulation_API.pdf)
