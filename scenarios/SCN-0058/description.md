# SESA-Lab staged real-time and hardware-in-the-loop grid demonstration

Demonstrates how mosaik can extend a software-only feeder simulation with an OPAL-RT network and a physical PV converter in three stages.

Figure 2 shows the complete arrangement. PYPOWER represents a low-voltage feeder with household and PV profiles. OPAL-RT adds higher voltage levels and a second low-voltage feeder, and a physical PV converter is connected from the AIT laboratory. The later SmartEST–SESA publication is also catalogued as SCN-0053. [PDF pp. 4–5, Section IV and Figures 2–4](https://doi.org/10.1109/SmartGridComm.2014.7007645)
