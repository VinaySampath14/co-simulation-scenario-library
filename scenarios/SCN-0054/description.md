# Virtual power plant rescheduling test over OPC UA

Tests an OPC UA interface between mosaik and a multi-agent system by monitoring whether a virtual power plant needs to reschedule its units.

Mosaik simulates generators and storage with changing reliability. JADE agents assess the coalition and determine whether rescheduling is needed. The paper reports step execution time for different numbers of units and agents. [PDF pp. 5–6, Section V-D and Figures 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1109/IECON.2013.6700395)
