# OpenDSS integration benchmark for a smart distribution grid

Evaluates the integration of OpenDSS with mosaik by comparing a smart-grid co-simulation against a native OpenDSS benchmark.

The setup exchanges power and control values with OpenDSS. The paper reports voltage profiles and execution times, including timings for the individual components. [PDF pages 3–6, Section 3.1 and Figures 7 and associated setup](https://ojs.sba.org.br/index.php/cba/article/view/6555)

The benchmark uses the IEEE 34-bus feeder, with PV at buses 830 and 848 and storage at bus 834. [Paper, Section 4.1 and Figure 5](https://ojs.sba.org.br/index.php/cba/article/view/6555)
