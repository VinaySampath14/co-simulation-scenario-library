# Air photovoltaic-thermal collectors coupled to a building heat pump

Uses mosaik to compare a photovoltaic-thermal collector coupled to an air-source heat pump with a PV-only baseline for building heating and domestic hot water.

The photovoltaic-thermal collector model supplies warmed inlet air and electrical output. The study compares collector configurations and evaluates heat-pump efficiency for building thermal demand. [PDF pp. 1–2, 5 and 8, abstract, component models and co-simulation setup](https://doi.org/10.18086/eurosun.2024.02.09)
