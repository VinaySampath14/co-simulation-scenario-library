# Reinforcement-learning integration for coordinated EV charging

Tests ways to integrate a learning controller into mosaik for coordinated EV charging while limiting demand peaks.

The case couples a small distribution grid, households, EV charging stations and a management system. A TF-Agents controller changes the charging power available to stations. The authors compare architectures for coupling the controller and the orchestrator. [PDF pp. 11–14, Section 5 and case-study setup](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-26236-4_21)
