# Synthetic data exchange through a simulated ICT network

The authors use mosaik to connect a synthetic data sender, the rettij network simulator, and a receiver. They compare values produced by the sender with values collected by the receiver to demonstrate data exchange through the co-simulation interface.

Section 5 reports the implemented communication test. The sender and receiver use synthetic values. The paper discusses coupling rettij with a power-flow simulator separately as a potential application.
