# SCN-0006 — Electrification of residential end-users

The chapter's first example asks how household electrification and flexibility affect distribution-grid operation. It considers heat pumps, EV charging and PV. SimCo-Energy represents technology adoption, demod generates electricity demand, and SIMONA represents grid operation. The chapter identifies household characterization and consistency between population, technology penetration and grid capacity as coupling requirements. [Chapter §4.1, pp. 483–484](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-34920-1_38)

The published design has demod generate activity profiles at 10-minute resolution and electrical load profiles at one-minute resolution. The aggregator supplies 30-minute average household consumption to SIMONA and monthly per-device energy consumption to SimCo-Energy. The record captures these proposed exchanges. The exact ports, common run horizon, grid case and executable configuration remain unresolved. The three hypotheses concern grid volatility, uptake of low-carbon technologies and congestion risk. The chapter frames the outcomes for this example as intended insights. [Chapter §4.1, pp. 483–484](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-34920-1_38)
