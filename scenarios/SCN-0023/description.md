# Electrolyser deployment and congestion in the German extra-high-voltage grid

Studies how the siting and operation of large-scale electrolysers affect load congestion in a German extra-high-voltage grid. The mosaik showcase compares operating strategies across two future grid cases.

The extra-high-voltage showcase combines SimBench grid and profile data with distributed electrolysers. It compares operation driven by renewable generation, electricity prices or maximum current, and varies the location of the electrolysers. The medium-voltage showcase is documented separately because the network and the research question differ.
