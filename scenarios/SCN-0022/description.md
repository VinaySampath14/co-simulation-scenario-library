# Economic emission dispatch for a Danish industrial greenhouse

Uses a Danish industrial greenhouse to study how a closed-loop dispatch controller can balance energy cost and emissions. The authors compare requirements in mosaik with those of operational deployment.

The control system computes setpoints for the greenhouse energy system in response to its state. The local energy system includes a gas boiler, a heat pump, district heating, combined heat and power, thermal storage and grid electricity. The practical implementation uses a separate operational architecture.
