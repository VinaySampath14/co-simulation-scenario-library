# Edge data center flexibility across single and federated sites

Compares how workload shifting, storage, renewable generation and waste heat recovery affect cost and peak demand across single-site and federated edge data centers.

The study compares staged configurations ranging from workload shifting at one Oldenburg site to coordinated operation across three sites in Oldenburg and Munich. The authors evaluate operating cost, peak load, renewable self-consumption and waste heat utilization across the configurations. [Source, Section V and Table 4](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3728082)

The paper cites a public PV simulator and workload traces. The integrated study source was not located.
