# NeMoLand commercial EV fleets and local photovoltaic generation

Investigates how commercial EV fleets and local generation affect power balance and substation loading across representative low-voltage grids.

The NeMoLand project used mosaik at OFFIS to compose variants of four representative low-voltage grids. The thesis describes a fleet charge controller that can limit charging when the local substation reaches its operating limits and prints the MoSL scenario specification in Appendix J.4. [Source, Sections 12.1.1 and 12.4.6.1, and Appendix J.4](https://oops.uni-oldenburg.de/1768/1/schsim13.pdf)

Appendix J.4 prints the MoSL scenario specification. A public executable package with models and inputs was not located.
