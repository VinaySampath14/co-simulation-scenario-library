# Distributed PV and storage agents on a low-voltage feeder

Demonstrates mosaik?PADE integration by simulating household demand, distributed PV and agent-controlled storage on a low-voltage grid.

The study uses 37 residential loads, 20 PV generators and 20 storage agents. PYPOWER computes grid conditions. Solar generation is supplied from CSV measurement data, while the PADE storage agents apply a simplified rule based on PV output. The paper is published under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
