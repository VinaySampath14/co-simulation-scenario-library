# Virtual power plant scheduling to prevent transformer overload

Evaluates how a technical virtual power plant can schedule flexible assets to relieve transformer congestion caused by simultaneous wind generation.

Three wind turbines and two flexible industrial loads are coordinated by the ISAAC control system. The grid also contains inflexible households and industrial loads. Section 3 reports the demonstration and its uncertainty analysis. [PDF pp. 10–14, Section 3 and Figure 4](https://doi.org/10.3390/app9050923)
