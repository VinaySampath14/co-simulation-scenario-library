# Transient stability of a grid-forming wind power plant

Benchmarks a mosaik and FMI co-simulation of a grid-forming wind turbine against a monolithic PowerFactory reference.

The authors separate a Simulink wind turbine controller from the PowerFactory power system, package the two as FMUs, and compare transient responses across configurations. The setup diagram shows the wind terminal, grid connection, and other generating units. [PDF pp. 6–10 and 15–22, Figures 4–5 and results](https://doi.org/10.3390/app11052410)
