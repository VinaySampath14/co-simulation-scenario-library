# ISAAC scheduling of smart buildings with household adaptation costs

Uses mosaik to couple smart building models with ISAAC and compare power schedules while accounting for household adaptation costs.

The building model aggregates PV, battery storage and EV charging. It sends default and alternative schedules to ISAAC along with a monetary estimate of the adaptation involved. The study compares different representative days. [PDF p. 10 onward, single-day simulation setup and results](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42162-019-0080-4)
