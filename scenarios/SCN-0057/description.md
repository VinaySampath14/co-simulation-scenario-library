# Ensemble uncertainty quantification in a PV and refrigerator feeder

Demonstrates ensemble-based uncertainty quantification in a mosaik feeder with photovoltaic panels and refrigerator loads.

The four-node low-voltage grid has one PV panel and two refrigerators at each node. Weather data drive PV production. The authors vary assumptions about weather, PV and refrigerator models and report ranges and quantiles for active power flow.
