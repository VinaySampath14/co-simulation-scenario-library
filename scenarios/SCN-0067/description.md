# Teaching distributed scheduling with controllable energy units

Describes a mosaik teaching exercise in which students build controllable energy models and compare centralized and distributed planning.

The exercise starts with a refrigerator model and adds generation, a metering authority, grid simulation, and a planning authority. Students progress from ordinary unit behaviour to scheduled control and compare a simulated-annealing approach with an agent-based one. The chapter describes an exercise sequence and leaves numerical study results open. [PDF pp. 5 and 8–9, Section 4.2 and Figure 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-658-20380-1_14)
