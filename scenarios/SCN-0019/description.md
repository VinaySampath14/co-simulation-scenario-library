# Residential EV charging strategies on a Dutch-style distribution grid

Compares EV charging strategies in a residential distribution-grid co-simulation. The authors measure voltage quality and asset loading alongside driver charging delay and cost.

The authors use a modified suburban Kerber grid in pandapower. Python EV models use charging behaviour derived from Dutch public-charger data. Household demand, PV output and electricity prices enter as time series. Mosaik synchronizes the components and the grid power-flow calculation.

The record is based on the PDF attached in Zotero. [PDF pages 1–3, Abstract and Section III](https://doi.org/10.1109/sest53650.2022.9898417)
