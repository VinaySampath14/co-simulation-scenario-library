# Wind turbine storm control benchmark in mosaik and HLA

Compares mosaik and HLA time synchronization by simulating wind turbine storm control under comparable coupling schemes.

The example couples a wind turbine generator plant and an active pitch controller. The authors compare how the two implementations reproduce the controller response as wind conditions push rotor speed past a threshold. The HLA implementation uses CERTI. [PDF pp. 5–8, Section 4 and Figure 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00450-017-0379-y)
