# Architecture-generated EV charging on a residential PV feeder

Demonstrates the transfer of an EV charging design from the SGAM Toolbox to a mosaik simulation of a residential feeder.

The setup includes household demand, PV, a price signal generator and EVs with a price-based charging strategy exported as Java code. Results are retained in HDF5 and displayed in the web visualization. [PDF pp. 12–16, case study and Figures 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42162-019-0084-0)
