# EV hosting capacity in the Am Ölper Berge residential district

Studies how increasing electric vehicle adoption affects voltage in the Am Ölper Berge low-voltage grid in Brunswick, Germany. It compares charging and local control strategies.

The district co-simulation uses a pandapower representation of the local grid and EV mobility and charging profiles derived using emobpy. The study models building energy management and grid correction, and compares voltage behaviour across EV uptake and control variants.

The record is based on the PDF attached in Zotero. [PDF pages 3–5, Sections 3.1–3.2 and Figure 2](https://doi.org/10.1049/icp.2022.2713)
