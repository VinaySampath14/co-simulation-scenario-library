# Battery model comparison in a simulated data center microgrid

Compares four battery models in a two-day Vessim data center experiment orchestrated with mosaik.

The simplified data center combines two constant-load server nodes, solar production driven by Berlin irradiance data, and interchangeable battery models. The study compares battery response under changing net power and examines the trade-off between modelling detail and runtime. The paper lists Odej Kao as an author.
