# SCN-0007 — Electrification of industrial prosumers

The chapter's second example studies the co-evolution of industrial demand for energy storage and the firms that develop it, with government intervention as a policy lever. ALIEN represents industrial energy management and storage adoption, SKIQ represents storage technology suppliers, and the SimCo governance module represents interventions under specified conditions. The chapter describes mosaik setting initial conditions and relaying offers and purchase or requirement information. It also notes that the coupled SKIQ decisions rely on actual purchases because the simplified industrial agents lack forward-looking requirements. [Chapter §4.2, pp. 484–486](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-34920-1_38)

The chapter qualitatively reports regions of the ALIEN–SKIQ parameter space where desirable technologies become dominant at an acceptable rate, with intervention needed in some cases. The runtime configuration, exact values, aggregation rule, and implementation artifacts remain unresolved. The record distinguishes reported conceptual exchanges from executable interfaces. [Chapter §4.2, pp. 485–486](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-34920-1_38)
