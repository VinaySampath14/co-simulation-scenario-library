# Cross-laboratory PV inverter and distribution-grid co-simulation

Demonstrates distributed smart-grid validation by coupling the SmartEST and SESA laboratories in mosaik and observing how emulated PV generation affects a low-voltage network.

The test case takes active and reactive power from the PV inverter at SmartEST and transfers those measurements into the mosaik setup. The paper reports the voltage of a PYPOWER node and the inverter profile, with results stored in HDF5. [PDF pp. 5–6, Section V and Figures 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIE.2015.7281633)
