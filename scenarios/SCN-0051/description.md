# Socio-technical residential demand with heat pump integration

Uses mosaik to assess how household behaviour and heating equipment combine to shape residential energy demand, and measures runtime as the number of households increases.

The authors couple demod occupancy and demand models with a TESPy-based heat pump, a hot-water tank and a heating controller. The paper reports household demand profiles and compares runtime for stand-alone demand simulation, mosaik integration and the added heating system.
