# Durlach district energy center validation with operational data

Validates the mosaik-based DES.sim framework against one year of operational data from the Durlach residential district in Karlsruhe.

The thesis models two energy center configurations, one based on a heat pump and one on combined heat and power. The comparison uses recorded operation to assess how well the simulation reproduces long-term energy balances and component performance.
