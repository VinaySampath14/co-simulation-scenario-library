# Office energy demand with stochastic occupancy and controls

Demonstrates a modified mosaik and FMI framework by coupling an office energy model with a stochastic occupant model.

The hypothetical office uses EnergyPlus for the building and No-MASS for occupant behaviour. The authors compare annual stochastic runs with deterministic schedules and a previous study. The office uses Geneva weather data. [PDF pp. 3–7, framework implementation, Section 4.2 and Figure 6](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.proeng.2017.07.092)
