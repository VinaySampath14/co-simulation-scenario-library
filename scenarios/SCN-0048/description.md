# eELib building self-consumption with forecast-based control

Demonstrates forecast-based home energy management in a mosaik simulation of a single prosumer building.

The eELib building case models electricity and heat demand, PV generation, a stationary battery, an electric vehicle and charger, and a heat pump. The home energy management system uses ideal forecasts and mathematical optimization to schedule flexible equipment and increase self-consumption.
