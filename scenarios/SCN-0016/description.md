# Wind-powered electric vehicle charging for architecture-to-simulation comparison

The case study describes an electric vehicle and charging station that can buy lower-priced energy from a wind turbine. A smart meter manages information exchange, and the authors use PYPOWER to represent the grid. Figure 2 also shows an energy market as a price source. Section 4.2 identifies dedicated mosaik models for the vehicle, charging station, smart meter, and wind turbine. [Paper, Sections 3 and 4.2](https://www.en-trust.at/papers/Peter24a.pdf)

The authors represent the same case in the Smart Grid Architecture Model (SGAM) Toolbox and in mosaik. Their comparison finds that the type-level architecture leaves out details about individual model instances needed for a complete co-simulation. They propose using the architectural model to generate starter code. [Paper, Abstract and Sections 4–5](https://www.en-trust.at/papers/Peter24a.pdf)

The paper lists CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for the publication and includes an example vehicle model listing. A public repository for the complete case study and code reuse terms remain unverified. [Conference paper](https://doi.org/10.5220/0012739800003714)

The companion chapter identifies IEEE 13-node test feeder data for its PYPOWER configuration of the case. [Companion chapter, Section 4.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-23187-1_2)
