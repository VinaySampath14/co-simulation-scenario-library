# Power-flow simulator exchangeability in a residential PV grid

Compares interchangeable power-flow simulators in a mosaik residential-grid study with household demand and PV generation.

The study connects the same household and PV time-series simulators to alternative power-flow backends. It examines how grid models and simulation interfaces affect the comparison. [PDF pages 2–5, power-flow interfaces and experimental setup](https://doi.org/10.1109/MSCPES.2015.7115410)
