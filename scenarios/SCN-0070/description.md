# Smart-grid test bench data for load scheduling and forecasting

Demonstrates the use of mosaik with a smart-grid teaching test bench to examine measurements, power demand and scheduling outputs.

The authors use test-bench data and software-generated outputs for co-simulation. Their examples plot induction-motor RMS voltage, aggregate demand, scheduled power demand, and forecast demand. The published figures establish the reported outputs while the full executable coupling remains unverified. [PDF pp. 24–28, Figures 24–27](https://doi.org/10.3390/en15072702)
