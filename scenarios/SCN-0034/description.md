# Behind-the-meter flexibility for self-consumption and grid congestion management

Tests how household energy assets can support both local self-consumption and distribution-grid congestion management in mosaik.

The study uses rural and semi-urban SimBench networks as variants. Household energy management systems schedule assets, while edge devices aggregate flexibility and coordinate with the distribution system operator. The authors compare stacked and single-use control approaches. [PDF pp. 3–4 and 6–9, Sections 3–5 and Figure 2](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4415340)

The companion experiment compares single-use and combined operation on rural and semi-urban SimBench low-voltage feeders. [PDF pp. 4–6, Sections 3.5–4 and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1049/icp.2022.3326)
