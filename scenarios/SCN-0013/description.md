# Smart-grid digital twin for cybersecurity data generation

The authors use mosaik to coordinate a pandapower grid simulation and a container-based communication network. Virtual terminal units exchange measurements and commands, and traffic recording provides data for studying smart-grid cybersecurity. [Preprint, Section IV-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.03979v1#S4.SS1)

The authors evaluate the data generation setup by comparing virtual network captures with measurements from a physical laboratory. They report differences in background traffic and greater consistency between virtual runs. They also demonstrate the same framework on a CIGRE medium-voltage benchmark topology, recorded here as a study variant. [Preprint, Sections V–VI and Figure 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.03979v1#S5)

The source record preserves the conference DOI and the versioned open preprint. The revision and availability of the complete testbed implementation remain unverified.
