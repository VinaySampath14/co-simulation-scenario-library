# Smart Nord feeder comparison of PV profiles and FMI models

Demonstrates mosaik’s FMI integration by replacing historical PV profiles with PV models in the same Smart Nord low-voltage grid.

The study combines a PYPOWER grid, household load profiles and a GridSurfer EV simulator. The PV variants use CSV measurement data or OpenModelica FMUs. The paper compares power at the local transformer. [PDF pp. 9–10, Section 7 and Figures 8–10](https://doi.org/10.1515/auto-2014-1087)
